Your shopping workflow

From discovery
to a better decision.

A useful list answers practical questions. What is the item? Will it fit? What evidence is available? What could the final cost be?

Keep original listing facts separate from your own estimates and preferences. Recheck the listing when you are ready to buy.

Start a CAFANS shortlist ↗
  1. Choose a category and purpose.

    Start with the item you need and how you plan to wear it. Use the category links above to focus your browsing.

  2. Record the exact product variant.

    Save the listing reference, color, size and visible specifications. Avoid mixing information from different variants.

  3. Review measurements and available photos.

    Compare measurements with something you already own. QC photos can help assess visible details but cannot prove authenticity or durability.

  4. Compare costs and seller terms.

    Check current prices, delivery estimates, applicable fees and return conditions. Treat unconfirmed shipping and import costs as estimates.

  5. Recheck before proceeding.

    Confirm availability and outstanding questions on the current listing. A saved spreadsheet entry can become outdated.

Clear answers

Before you browse.

Quick explanations of what this guide provides and what to verify on a product listing.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. Entries may include categories, sizes, listed prices and photo references. The list itself does not verify product quality, authenticity or current availability.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a category directory and an educational shopping checklist. Category links open CAFANS; no downloadable spreadsheet or live Oopbuy inventory is provided.

What should I check before choosing a product?

Check the current listing, product measurements, materials, available variants, seller terms and total estimated cost. Review available QC photos for visible details, while recognizing that photos cannot prove authenticity or long-term durability.

How do I compare the total cost of two finds?

Compare the item price, domestic delivery, applicable service fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties. Use current quotes and distinguish estimates from confirmed charges.

Are the featured brands affiliated with this guide?

No brand affiliation or endorsement is claimed. The brand-themed artwork is illustrative and does not establish product availability, authenticity or authorization.

Where do the shopping links lead?

General shopping links lead to https://www.cafans.com. Category links lead to their corresponding CAFANS category pages.
